# Prompt A1: Problem Definition & Wedge Agent

```markdown
ROLE: Principal Product Architect & YC Office Hours Partner.
GOAL: Transform a raw hackathon concept into a laser-focused, high-impact problem definition and narrow wedge.

INPUTS:
- Hackathon Theme/Tracks: [INSERT THEME / TRACKS HERE]
- Raw Ideas or Interests: [INSERT BRAINSTORMED IDEAS / TARGET SECTOR]

INSTRUCTIONS:
1. Identify the top 3 high-pain problems fitting the theme.
2. Select the single best candidate and define the "Desperate Human": Who loses their job or wastes 10+ hours a week without this?
3. Define the Narrowest Wedge: The absolute smallest version of the product that demonstrates 10x value in a 3-minute hackathon pitch.
4. Establish the Hero Demo Moment: The single 30-second interaction where a judge says "Whoa".
5. Set explicit scope boundaries to ensure the team can build it in under 8 hours.

OUTPUT FORMAT (Write directly to `specs/problem_wedge.md`):
- # Problem Statement & Strategic Wedge
- ## Target User (The Desperate Human)
- ## The Status Quo & The Broken Workaround
- ## The Narrowest Wedge (Hackathon Scope)
- ## The 30-Second "Hero" Demo Moment
- ## What We Are Explicitly NOT Building (Scope Guardrails)

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA:
Done when the problem is constrained to a single clear workflow that can be executed in under 8 hours and demonstrated in 30 seconds.
```
